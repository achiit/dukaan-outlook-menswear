From the owner

A shop built for our own neighbourhood

My elder cousin used to say a man should walk out of a shop feeling taller than he walked in. That line stayed with me through the years I spent buying clothes for family weddings and wondering why good fits cost so much more outside Jehanabad.

So we opened Outlook at Malahchak, with a trial room, bright shelves, and prices I would be happy to pay myself. If a shirt does not sit right, I will tell you so before you buy it.
